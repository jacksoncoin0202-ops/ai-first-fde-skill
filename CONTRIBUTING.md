# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they improve practical AI field deployment, East Asian adoption understanding, troubleshooting depth, or public-safe delivery templates.

## Contribution rules

1. Keep examples anonymized.
2. Do not add customer secrets or identifiable deployment details.
3. Keep the canonical meaning aligned across Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese docs.
4. Prefer operational checklists and repeatable workflows over abstract opinions.
5. Every new module should include at least one eval case.

## Quality bar

A contribution should help an agent ask better questions, design safer systems, deploy with fewer surprises, or diagnose field problems faster.
