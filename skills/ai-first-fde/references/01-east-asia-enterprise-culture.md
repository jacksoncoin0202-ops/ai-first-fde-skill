# East Asian Enterprise Culture Notes

This module helps the FDE avoid blindly applying Western SaaS adoption assumptions.

## Recurring patterns

- Hierarchy: people may wait for senior approval before trying tools.
- Face-saving: direct criticism can stop truthful feedback.
- Consensus: formal meetings often ratify decisions shaped beforehand.
- Risk avoidance: users fear responsibility for AI mistakes.
- Seniority: experienced staff may feel their know-how is being extracted.
- Hidden resistance: teams may agree publicly while continuing old workflows.
- Informal power centers: family businesses and long-running departments may have decision-makers outside the org chart.

## Design responses

- Use no-blame pilots.
- Position senior staff as reviewers and quality owners.
- Start with team metrics.
- Protect existing status while changing workflow.
- Use low-friction channels where appropriate.
- Build confidence through small wins.
- Align managers privately before formal rollout.
